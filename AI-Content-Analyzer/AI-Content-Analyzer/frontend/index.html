About these results

No AI-detection method can reliably determine whether a human or a model wrote a text — including this one. Results are linguistic and statistical indicators, never proof of authorship, and no accuracy percentage is claimed. Rewriting is for clarity and readability; it is not a guarantee against any external detector.
